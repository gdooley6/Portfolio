Josh Shelton

Dedicated to preserving those special memories with a cinematic look.

I’m a Media Production graduate from FSU who found my calling behind the lens. I’ve filmed everything from college football to national tours, worked with Nat Geo and Discovery, and captured countless weddings. Off the clock, I’m either with my wife and son or deep in the woods, camera in hand.

Contact Us

At Shelton Pictures quality means everything. By utilizing techniques learned on set while working for network television programs we provide cinematic lighting for all of our portrait sessions in the Pensacola, Florida area.
